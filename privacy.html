Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the Android apps R53 Logger - Flasher (com.geekopolis.r53logger) and R53 Coding (com.geekopolis.r53code) handle information. Both are tools for working with a MINI R53 (W11) vehicle over a USB K+DCAN adapter (and, for the logger, an optional ESP32 Bluetooth LE wideband bridge).

Summary

Vehicle and coding data stay on your device unless you choose to share a file, or choose to upload a log to Geekopolis Logs (described below). Google Play may process purchase and integrity information when you use paid features, under Google’s policies.

Information the apps store on your device

  • Telemetry logs (Logger): CSV logs of engine / sensor data you record (RPM, temperatures, AFR, etc.). Stored in app-private storage.
  • ECU / coding backups: Binary or coding backups you create when reading from the car. Stored locally for restore and share.
  • Settings: Preferences such as theme, units, poll rates, wideband calibration, and unlock / purchase cache flags.

The apps never upload these files on their own. You can export or share them yourself (for example through the Android share sheet), and the Logger can upload a log you pick to Geekopolis Logs.

Uploading a log to Geekopolis Logs (Logger)

Share log link in the Logger's graph screen uploads one saved log to logs.geekopolis.com, a site run by Geekopolis, so it can be viewed and analysed there. Nothing is uploaded until you confirm, and a log that is still recording can't be uploaded.

  • Who can see it: anyone with the log's link can view and download the whole file. The link isn't listed or searchable, but treat it like the file itself. A log can carry your VIN or other vehicle identifiers if they were recorded in it.
  • What the site keeps: the log file and its title and notes, which app and version sent it, when, and a one-way hash of the network address it came from, used only to limit abuse. The site doesn't keep your address itself.
  • Checking your purchase: uploads come with the unlock. The first time you upload, the app sends the site your Google Play purchase receipt (order number, product, purchase token and app name, signed by Google) so the site can confirm the purchase. The site keeps the order number and a one-way hash of the purchase token, and gives the app an access code it stores and sends with each upload. If Matt gave you an access code instead, that code is sent.
  • Editing and deleting: after an upload the app saves a private edit key on your phone. Manage upload opens the log on the site, where that key lets you change its title and notes or delete it. Deleted logs stop being available straight away. Email matt@geekopolis.com and an upload, or everything tied to your purchase, will be removed.

Uploads aren't sold, shared or used for advertising. They're used to show you your log and, if you ask for help, to look at it with you.

Device connections (not sent to us)

  • USB serial (K+DCAN): Used only to talk to the vehicle modules. Traffic stays between the phone and the car.
  • Bluetooth LE (Logger wideband): Connects to your ESP32 wideband bridge to receive sensor voltage. No cloud relay.
  • On older Android versions, location permission may be required by the OS for BLE scanning; the apps do not use GPS for mapping or tracking.

Internet use

Internet access is used only for:

  • Google Play Billing — one-time unlock purchases and purchase restoration.
  • Geekopolis Logs (Logger), only when you upload a log: the purchase check and the upload described above.
  • Google Play Integrity (Logger, when enabled) — the app may request an integrity token from Google and send that token (plus a nonce and package name) to a developer-configured verify endpoint. The token is for app/device integrity, not a profile of your driving.

We do not operate analytics SDKs, ad networks, or crash-reporting backends in these apps. We do not sell personal information.

Accounts and children

The apps do not create accounts with us. Purchases use your Google account through Play. The apps are not directed at children under 13.

Third parties

Google Play services process billing and (where used) integrity attestation according to Google’s Privacy Policy. If you share a log or backup with another app or website, that destination’s policy applies. Geekopolis Logs is covered by this policy.

Data retention and deletion

Local logs, backups, and settings remain until you delete them in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall. Uninstalling removes app-private data on the device. Logs uploaded to Geekopolis Logs stay until you delete them with their edit key or ask us to. Play purchase records are retained by Google as part of your Google account.
